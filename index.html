Founded by Himaly Raj Sen Sid, working across Rajasthan and beyond.

An editorial eye,
trained on the quiet moments of a wedding.

Photowalla approaches every wedding the way a magazine approaches a cover story — composed, considered, unhurried. Light is read before it is used. A room is understood before it is photographed.

Working out of Bhilwara, Jaipur and Udaipur, the studio takes on a limited number of weddings each season, choosing depth of attention over volume — so that every family that works with Photowalla receives a body of work that feels considered, not assembled.

Himaly Raj Sen Sid, Founder
