Photowalla was built on a simple idea — that a wedding deserves to be documented the way a story is told, not the way an event is logged. Every frame is composed with the same care an editor gives a magazine cover: considered light, honest emotion, nothing rushed.

Working out of Bhilwara, Jaipur and Udaipur, Photowalla brings a distinctly modern, editorial sensibility to Rajasthan's rich wedding traditions — celebrating families who want their biggest day held with the same warmth they felt living it.
